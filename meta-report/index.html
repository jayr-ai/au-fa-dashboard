Action list

  1. Turn on Advantage+ creative across the top 10 ads. Meta's highest-value opportunity (+4 points) estimates up to 53% lower cost per result from standard enhancements, and the account already leans on Reels, so this is the lowest-effort win available right now.
  2. Watch the funnel mix. July ran masterclass lead-gen heavy (510 website leads at A$38.63 blended, plus 65 front-end purchases at A$361.01); the earlier June model leaned on paid challenge and application purchases. Early August has opened on pure lead-gen again. Confirm with the client which funnel is actually closing high-ticket sales before scaling one over the other.
  3. Reconcile against the CRM. Because the high-ticket close is offline, pull booked-call and closed-sale numbers from the client's CRM and divide by spend to get true cost per booked call and cost per sale. That is the number that should drive budget, not pixel ROAS.
  4. Scale the proven creative winners. "Reel: $100k Side Hustle" is the standout lead driver (A$25.26 per lead in July, 273 leads); "Reel: Best Side Hustle", "Stacie 2: Kids" and "Stacie 1: Netflix" also carry lead volume, while "Ad 2 | Teacher" and "Podcast Ad 3/4" drove the front-end purchases. Feed budget to these angles.
  5. Fix conversion optimisation and Reels specs. Meta flags optimising for the Maximize-conversions goal (17% lower cost per result) and adding 9:16 sound-on video to Reels ads (8% lower). Both are quick structural fixes.
  6. Lean into the 55+ audience. Across every window the 55-64 and 65+ brackets deliver the highest CTR at competitive CPC; they are under-weighted on spend versus 35-54. Test shifting budget older.
  7. Prune the Sandbox and low-return test campaigns. "Sandbox | 3 Day Challenge" spent A$604.91 in July with zero tracked purchases and "Application Funnel | Purchases" spent A$931.06 for a single purchase; cap or cut them and redirect to the working challenge and masterclass funnels.
  8. Keep delivery in the strong AU states. NSW, VIC and QLD carry the volume at healthy CTR; the account is already well-distributed, so hold that and monitor frequency (currently ~1.3 to 2.8).
